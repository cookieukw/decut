# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gameplay creators who synchronize video, game audio and voice in Kdenlive, then use decut to prepare an edited OTIO for further work in Kdenlive.

## Product Purpose

Review and remove pauses across linked track groups without rendering intermediate video. Preserve source media, speech boundaries and synchronization.

## Brand Personality

Practical, technical, direct.

## Anti-references

Do not recreate a full video editor. Avoid redundant import buttons, explanatory banners occupying the timeline, cramped track lists, decorative typography and operations that silently delete audio.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize tracks and review over settings.
- Preview must use the same decisions as the export.
- Make destructive changes explicit and reversible until confirmed.
- Keep long-media processing outside the UI thread with bounded memory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain native Qt focus and keyboard controls. Show timecodes and numeric settings alongside color highlights. Support Portuguese and English. No additional requirements have been specified.
